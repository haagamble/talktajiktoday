Flash cards and Pictures

  • 8 weather flash cards from SparkleBox
  • 24 parts of the body flash cards from SparkleBox
  • ESL flashcards has lots of good sets of flash cards - school objects, daily activities, adjectives and many more.
  • Alifbo, the first grader reader is online in PDF form. While not a great book for learning Tajik, this new edition has lots of great pictures in the first 23 pages - portraits of important Tajik people, and pictures of typical Tajik life, and also a colourful alphabet chart. It would be great to print these pictures for use in lessons.

Lesson plans and self-study arranged (more or less) from easy to difficult

  • My Family a lesson plan for beginner level learners
  • Past Tense Easy Worksheets, do on your own or with a language helper
  • Future Tense Easy Worksheets, do on your own or with a language helper
  • Pronunciation Practice, for those unusual letters.
  • Double Consonants, a pronunciation exercise.
  • Sentence Drills with To Go - lots of drills to practice with a language helper using the verb рафтан, to go. For beginners, but there are ideas at the end to use the drills with more complex sentences.

  • My Family, an intermediate lesson plan.
  • Lesson on Adjectives, Intermediate level
  • Substitution Drills, To Want - practicing with хостан.
