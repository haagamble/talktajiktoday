Texts for Tajik Language Learning

  • Tajik Persian: Readings in History, Culture and Society - textbook to help Tajik language learners to develop reading fluency, by Razi Ahmad. Book can be downloaded as a PDF

Tajik School Textbooks

Some books on these sites are the same.
  • Library 2030: This is the Department of Education site for library books. There are textbooks in all subjects. To find stories, apply the filters интихоби забон: тоҷикӣ and мавзӯҳо: афсона. Here are a few.
    • Бузаки Ҷингилапо (PDF). The curly-footed Kid Goat.
    • Моҳича ва Қурбоққа (PDF). The little Fish and the Frog. This one is in Tajik and English.
    • Зоғак ва Ғозак, (PDF). The little Crow and the little Goose.
  • Library of Tajik textbooks: From this website you can download textbooks from grades 1 to 11 in all subjects.
    • Intermediate learners - try grade 2 Санъат ва меҳнат (PDF) or Математика (PDF).
    • Advanced learners - how about grade 6 География (PDF) or grade 9 Биология (PDF)?
  • Tajik textbooks: This is the new site for the public library, and you can also download textbooks from here.

Tajik books

  • Over 30 Tajik books that have been translated from English into Tajik. Examples include:
    • More than a Carpenter (PDF), by Josh McDowell
    • Star of Light (PDF), by Patricia St. John, comic book
  • Tajik fiction from the public library. Tajik works of fiction that can be downloaded as PDFs.
  • Tajik Bible to read or listen online.
  • Electronic Bible Publications has links to ebooks and apps.
  • Аввалин Калимаҳои Китоби Муқаддаси Ман, My first Bible words. Short descriptions of words like ҳақиқат (truth), таърих (history), муҳаббат (love), and гуноҳ (sin).
  • Tajik library - a website with a collection of Tajik books that can be downloaded as PDFs. For example:
    • Ёддоштҳо, Memories, by Sadriddin Ayni. This is a collection of stories about the life of the author and his family.
    • Яке буд, яке набуд (афсонаҳо), A collection of Tajik fairy tales.
    • Ҳамадон Бош!, Be Knowledgeable! The Big Book of Questions and Answers. Like a kid's first encyclopedia.

Other

  • Essays - The public library has a collection of short essays including short autobiographies of famous Tajik people.
  • TED Talks - English with Tajik subtitles.
